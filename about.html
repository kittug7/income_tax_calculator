About

What this page is for.

TaxRoot is a front-end income tax planning experience. The landing page keeps the site simple, and the standalone calculator page handles detailed section-wise entry, comparison, and export.

Designed for clarity

  • Cleaner first screen instead of a long audit-style sheet.
  • Dedicated calculator page for focused data entry.
  • Separate support pages so users can read context without leaving the site flow.

Simple first view

The website opens with a clear introduction and direct pathways to the calculator, about, privacy, and tips pages.

Useful comparison

Users can quickly compare old and new regime estimates and understand which regime appears lighter for the current inputs.

Planning oriented

This is built for quick estimation and understanding, not as a full replacement for final filing or professional review.

Use TaxRoot to plan and compare. Recheck final filing details with current rules and supporting documents.
