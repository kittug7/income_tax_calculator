Tips

Practical tax planning reminders.

These tips are meant to support the calculator workflow before and after users enter their values.

Use these before filing

Run the calculator for planning, organize proof documents early, and recheck current rules before submitting a return.

1. Track early

Keep proofs organized

Store receipts, donations, investments, insurance proofs, and loan statements in one place during the year.

2. Review deductions

Use caps wisely

Do not assume every investment automatically reduces tax. Check deduction limits and regime relevance first.

3. Compare regimes

Run both scenarios

A stronger deduction profile often favors the old regime, while simpler income structures may fit the new regime better.

4. Recheck before filing

Estimate first, file carefully

Use calculators for planning, then verify the final return with current rules, official forms, and professional help when needed.

Tips help with planning, but the final return still needs current-rule verification.
